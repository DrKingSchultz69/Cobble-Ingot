# Research plan

Back to [[Research Home]]

**Type:** one-time snapshot (not continuous) · **Evidence window:** 2025-10-01 → **2026-10-08** · **Started:** 2026-10-08 · **Status:** day 1

## Research questions (answer these, nothing else)

1. What makes people **lose time or money** with coding agents and AI IDEs today?
2. Where do they **lose trust** (wrong edits, hidden cost, security, "it said done but it wasn't")?
3. What breaks when they use **more than one model or agent** (switching, context, limits, cost)?
4. How do the **non-developer roles** (designers, analysts, PMs, ML/data, ops) struggle differently?
5. What **workarounds** have people built, and what do those tell us about missing features?

## In scope
- Terminal coding agents (harnesses), AI IDEs, cloud agents, multi-agent/multi-model orchestrators, AI app builders.
- All roles in [[roles-and-tools]].

## Out of scope (for now)
- General chatbots not used for building software.
- Model quality benchmarks (we care about workflow pain, not leaderboard scores).

## Sprint (5 working days)

| Day | Done when |
|---|---|
| 1 | Questions confirmed; GitHub collector run; 10–20 report rows |
| 2–3 | All 12 listening sessions done |
| 3–4 | 1 in 5 auto rows checked; optional 3–6 calls done |
| 5 | Themes scored, ideas scored, decision made, **report.md written: research closed** |

## Targets
- **Pain log:** 300+ rows, every one linked and inside the evidence window.
- **Roles:** every role family has at least 15 rows (designers and analysts included).
- **Calls (optional):** 3–6, only to confirm or question the top problems.
- **Decision:** 1–3 features chosen, each backed by 20+ log rows from at least 3 roles.

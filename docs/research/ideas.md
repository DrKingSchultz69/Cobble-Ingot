## Step 2: Ideas (3+ per question, at least one only Netheron can do)

`Idea score = Impact × Confidence × Novelty ÷ Effort`
- **Impact (1–5):** how much of the logged pain it removes.
- **Confidence (1–5):** how sure the evidence makes us (5 = many rows and interviews).
- **Novelty (1–5):** 5 = no existing tool does it, or only Netheron could (cross-vendor, shared memory, phone…).
- **Effort (1–5):** 1 = an hour, 5 = weeks.

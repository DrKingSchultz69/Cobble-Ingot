# Interviews

Back to [[Research Home]]

Talk to **5 people per key role** (start with the top 3 in [[roles-and-tools]]). 20–30 minutes each, over a call or chat.

## Finding people
- Friends and colleagues in that role.
- People who posted a pain you logged: reply politely and ask for 20 minutes.
- Discord or Slack communities from [[sources]]: post once, honestly ("researching problems with AI coding tools, not selling anything").

## Script (ask about the past, never pitch)

**Warm-up (2 min)**
1. What's your role, and what did you build last week?
2. Which AI coding tools do you use, and which do you pay for?

**Last real time (15 min).** This is the core of the interview.
3. Tell me about the **last time** an AI tool frustrated you. What were you trying to do?
4. What happened next? What did you do about it?
5. How much time or money did it cost you?
6. Have you built or found a workaround (script, extension, prompt, another tool)?
7. When do you switch between tools or models? What's annoying about switching?
8. What do you double-check because you don't trust the AI's work?

**Role-specific (5 min)**: pick what fits:
- Designer: how does a design become code today? Where does it go wrong?
- DevOps / Cloud: would you let an agent run commands near production? What stops you?
- AI / ML: how do agents handle notebooks, long jobs, GPUs and evals for you?
- Analyst: how do you check that the AI's SQL or numbers are right?
- Lead / manager: how do you review AI-written PRs, and how do you track team AI costs?

**Wrap-up (3 min)**
9. If you could fix one thing about your AI tools tomorrow, what would it be?
10. Who else should I talk to?

**Don't:** describe Netheron's features, ask "would you use X?", or argue. Just listen and take notes.
